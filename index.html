    Privacy

    How your data is handled

    1. 01

      Selected

      Your PDF stays where it is on your computer. The page only gets permission to read it.

    2. 02

      Read in This Tab

      The statement is read in your browser's memory. Nothing is uploaded, not even a page.

    3. 03

      Checked and Downloaded

      Only the extracted rows stay on screen. Download Excel to keep a copy on your computer.

    4. 04

      Gone When You Leave

      Close the tab or clear the workbook and it's all removed. Nothing is stored in the browser.

    Your browser caches Parserje's reading tools so the page loads faster. That's code, not your data. Downloaded Excel files are saved like any other download.

    Don't take our word for it. Try the Unplug Test.

    1. Click Get Offline-Ready so Parserje loads its reading tools.
    2. Turn off your Wi-Fi or unplug your network.
    3. Convert a statement. It still works, because nothing is sent anywhere.
